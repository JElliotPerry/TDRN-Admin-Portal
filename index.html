Platform Capabilities

TDRN provides a complete disaster response infrastructure.

👥

Member Management

Onboard, certify, and manage responders with role-based access and real-time status tracking across all counties.

🚨

Incident Command

ICS-compliant incident management with team assignment, resource tracking, and real-time operational reporting.

🎓

Training Management

Schedule and track ICS, NIMS, CERT, and specialty certifications with automated compliance monitoring and renewal alerts.

🔧

Equipment Tracking

Full lifecycle inventory management — location, maintenance schedules, certification status, and deployment assignment.

📡

Communications

Integrated alert system with multi-channel notifications, HAM radio coordination, and priority messaging for all team levels.

📊

Readiness Scoring

Automated county and team readiness scores based on member certifications, equipment status, and training compliance metrics.

🤝

Partner Coordination

Manage mutual aid agreements, coordinate with local EMA, fire, EMS, Red Cross, and government agencies for unified response.

🗺

National Expansion

Scalable county-to-state-to-national architecture. Activate new counties and states with structured onboarding and replication tools.
